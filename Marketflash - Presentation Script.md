So, lets go deeper, because this is the most important chart in the whole dashboard. Every cell is a channel-audience combination. The darker the cell, the better it converts, and you can see already the dark blue on Instagram for adults between 40 and 60 years old is converting well at 1.53 %,,  and email targeting men 18 to 40 converts at 1.45% for under forty dollars per customer.

**Slide 05: Three Root Causes**

There are three root causes:

**Channel-Audience Mismatch:** YouTube is an entertainment platform. Seniors watching YouTube are not in a buying mindset. Putting conversion-focused ads there is like advertising skateboards on a golf channel. The audience is present — but the intent is not.

**Seasonal Budget Misalignment:** February and December are our two most expensive months by a significant margin. These are periods of low consumer intent and high market competition. We are spending the most at exactly the wrong time.

**Team Performance Gap:**Michael Camacho converts at 1.13%. Our bottom performers convert at 0.96%. An 18% gap between best and worst on the same team, with the same budget, points to a strategy and brief quality problem — not a market problem.  
 So what happens if we do not fix these three things? Let me show you.

 

**Slide 05: What Happens If We Change Nothing**

   
It will continue, because these are structural patterns. YouTube and Seniors 60 plus will keep converting at 0.63% because the mismatch has not changed. February will keep being expensive because the market competition has not changed. And the team performance gap will keep costing us output because nobody has shared what the best performers know. These are not risks — they are certainties, unless we make five specific moves that doesn’t cost any money, starting this quarter.

**Slide 06: Five Moves That Change the Game (bar chart)**

Five moves. Zero additional budget required. Everything I am about to show you is a reallocation or a timing change — not a request for more money. 

**Move 01:** shift YouTube budget to TikTok and Email. Same spend, thirteen percent better return.   
**Move 02:** stop YouTube targeting Seniors 60 plus — this week. It is our worst-performing combination by a wide margin and there is no good reason to keep running it.   
**Move 03:** move fifteen percent of our February and December budget forward to March and the summer months. We know March works. We keep underfunding it.   
**Move 04:** scale what we know works — Instagram and Email targeting the audiences that already convert at one-and-a-half percent.   
**Move 05:** document what our top performers do differently and give that knowledge to the whole team.
